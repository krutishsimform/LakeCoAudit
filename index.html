02 · Headline findings

The five things that matter most

Fixing these five themes captures the majority of the value. Each maps to specific reports and a defined effort.

CRITICAL

DirectQuery dominates

14 of 15 reports route every query to the source live. Migrating to Import unlocks 5×–50× faster dashboards.

Effort: 5–10 days · Impact: Suite-wide performance jump

CRITICAL

19 Many-to-Many cross-filters

M:M with bidirectional filtering in DirectQuery is the costliest relationship type. 12 of 15 reports have at least one. Inventory Turns and Scheduling have three each.

Effort: 2–3 days · Impact: 25–40% faster

CRITICAL

57 wrong-number bugs

Process Scrap PPM operator bug, Util % defaulting to 100% on /0, OVERALL EFFICIENCY duplicate, COSTYTD P-factor, and 53 more. Each shows incorrect figures in dashboards today.

Effort: ~1.5 days for the top 11 · Impact: Correctness

HIGH

5 reports use only bidirectional

FIFO/LIFO, Inventory Turns, OEE, Sales, and Scheduling have zero single-direction relationships. The most aggressive configuration in the suite.

Effort: 0.25 day each · Impact: 15–25% faster

STRENGTH

Foundations to preserve

Inventory on Import

Largest performance lever already pulled — sets the pattern for the other 14.

Purchasing's date dims

All 3 date relationships correctly OneDirection — the cleanest config in the suite.

Job Details fixes shipped

Duplicate fact removed, 47 measures fixed, all bidirectional flipped — already delivered with client consent.
